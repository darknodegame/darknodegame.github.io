Custom Field✕

Minusweeper — the rules are reversed. You may only click mines.

Your first click is always safe and opens an area. After that, every click must land on a mine. Click a safe square and you lose.

Numbers count the mines still live around a square. Each mine you click is defused, so the numbers around it drop — and any that reach zero open up new areas.

No flags. Defuse every mine to win.
